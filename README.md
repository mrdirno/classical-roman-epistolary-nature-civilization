## Entry Points for Contributors

**Start anywhere. Fork freely. No permission needed.**

### Story Entry Points

- Write a letter from Tertia to her father's ashes, explaining her choice to stay in Rome and confront its 'sickness'.
- Compose a missing chapter: a letter from Lepidus to a political ally, where he describes the 'peculiar woman from the hills' and her effect on his household.
- Write the journal entry of Thias, Tertia's slave, observing his mistress's work and the strange dynamics of the Palatine house.
- Create a 'field guide' entry from Tertia's notes: 'On the Humours of Cultivated Earth, and Their Distempers'.

### Technical Entry Points

- Build an interactive 'Epistolary Engine' that generates correspondence between characters based on story events and thematic tensions.
- Design a 'Soil Health Simulator' for the garden, modeling variables like water, sunlight, soil composition, and 'political pressure' affecting its recovery.
